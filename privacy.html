Privacy

Your information, plainly.

What we collect through this site, why, and how to opt out.

What we collect

When you use the price calculator, we save your inputs and the range it showed you. No name or contact details are attached unless you send a form.

When you send a form, we save what you type: your name, business name, work email, phone, the address of the space, when you'd like to start and any note. We also save the estimate you made and whether you ticked the box for emails, with the exact wording and the time.

To know which ads and pages work, we save how you found us: the page you landed on, the site you came from, and the campaign labels and click IDs that Google, Meta and Microsoft add to ad links. We keep these for 90 days in your browser and send them with any form.

Cookies and ad tools

This site uses Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Google Ads, and Meta's tools for Facebook and Instagram ads. They use cookies to count visits and to tell us when an ad led to an enquiry. If you send a form, a scrambled (hashed) copy of your email and phone may be shared with Google and Meta so they can match the enquiry to an ad. They can't read it back.

Visitors in Quebec, the EU, the UK and Switzerland start with ad and analytics cookies off.

How we use it

To answer you, price your space and set up a walkthrough. To see which ads and pages bring enquiries. We don't sell your information.

We only send you marketing emails if you ticked the box, and every one has a one-click way to stop.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to two years, then deleted, unless you become a client. Calculator results without contact details are kept for up to two years to understand demand.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. Send a note through the walkthrough form and mark it Privacy.
