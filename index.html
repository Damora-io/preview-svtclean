What a kitchen deep clean covers

  • Floors and drains degreased, tile and grout scrubbed
  • Under and behind the line: equipment pulled where it moves
  • Walls, shelving and prep tables washed down
  • Equipment outsides: ranges, fryers, fridges, the dish machine
  • Hood filters washed
