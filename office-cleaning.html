Every visit

One checklist, every visit.

We agree the list with you at the walkthrough. Then it's the same list, every time we come.

  • Work areas: desks and counters wiped, chairs set, phones and keyboards left where they are
  • Touch points: door handles, light switches, railings and elevator buttons
  • Washrooms: toilets, sinks, mirrors and partitions cleaned, dispensers refilled
  • Kitchenettes: counters, sinks, microwaves and fridge doors, dishes left in the rack
  • Floors: hard floors swept and mopped, carpets vacuumed
  • Glass: partitions and doors at hand height, spot cleaned
  • Bins: emptied, fresh liners, recycling sorted to your building's rules

Questions

Office cleaning, answered.

How much does office cleaning cost in Toronto?

Most offices pay by the month, based on size and how often the cleaners come. A 5,000 sq ft office cleaned 3 times a week usually runs a set monthly range, plus HST.

Washrooms, carpet and restocking move it a little. The calculator gives your range.

Is office cleaning the same as commercial cleaning?

Office cleaning is one kind of commercial cleaning. Commercial cleaning covers any business space: offices, clinics, shops, restaurant kitchens and new builds. We do all of them, and price each one on its own terms.

How often should an office be cleaned?

Most offices of 10 to 50 people do well with 3 visits a week. Busy offices and anywhere with lots of visitors need 5. A small office with a few people can start at 1 or 2.

Do we need to be there?

No. Most offices give us a key or door code and we clean after everyone leaves.

Who brings the supplies?

We bring the cleaning products and equipment. Paper towel, soap and bin liners can come from you, or we stock them for a small add-on you can see in the calculator.
