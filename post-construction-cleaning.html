Questions

Post-construction, answered.

What does post-construction cleaning cost in Toronto?

It's priced per square foot and by phase. A final clean usually runs a set rate per sq ft, plus HST. A 2,000 sq ft final clean comes to about a set range.

What is included in post-construction cleaning?

The rough clean takes out debris and bulk dust. The final clean details every surface: ledges, sills, frames, vents, fixtures, windows inside, floors, washrooms and kitchens. The touch-up is a last pass before handover.

Do you take away construction waste?

We remove dust and small debris. Bulk waste, drywall offcuts and bins stay with the contractor.

How soon can you start?

Tell us your handover date when you book. We plan the crew around it, and a rush within 72 hours is available.

What do you need on site?

Power, running water and access to the space. If other trades are still working, tell us which areas are ready.
