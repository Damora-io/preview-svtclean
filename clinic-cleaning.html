Questions

Clinic cleaning, answered.

Why does clinic cleaning cost more than office cleaning?

A clinic has more touch points, more surfaces that need disinfecting and a stricter routine. That takes longer per square foot, so clinics usually run about one and a half times an office of the same size.

Can you clean while we're closed?

Yes. Most clinics have us in after the last patient, so the rooms are ready for the morning.

Do you clean dental operatories?

We clean the room itself: counters, sinks, the outsides of the chair and equipment, and the floor. Instruments and anything your staff sterilize stay with your team.

What products do you use?

Disinfectants approved for use in Canada, used at the contact time on the label. We can match the products your clinic already uses.
