The deep clean

The places a daily wipe never reaches.

Your crew keeps the kitchen running. A deep clean takes care of the grease that builds up behind and under everything.

  • Floors and drains: degreased, tile and grout scrubbed, drains cleared of build-up
  • Under and behind the line: equipment pulled out where it moves
  • Walls and ceilings around the line washed down
  • Equipment outsides: ranges, fryers, ovens, fridges, the dish machine
  • Shelving and prep tables: stainless washed and polished
  • Hood filters: taken down, degreased and put back
Hoods and ducts

One thing we don't do.

Exhaust hood and duct interiors have to be cleaned by a certified hood cleaning company. That's not us.

What we do: wash the hood filters and the hood's outside, and work around your hood cleaner's visit. Ask at the walkthrough and we'll point you to one.

Questions

Kitchen cleaning, answered.

How often should a restaurant kitchen be deep cleaned?

Most restaurants book a deep clean every 1 to 3 months. A busy line or a fryer-heavy menu needs it more often. Your crew's daily cleaning keeps it there between visits.

How much does a commercial kitchen deep clean cost?

A typical restaurant with a full cook line runs a set range per deep clean, plus HST. Smaller and larger kitchens are in the calculator.

Do you clean at night?

Yes. We start after close and finish before your morning crew comes in.

Will a deep clean help with inspections?

It gets the places inspectors look at clean: under equipment, drains, walls and behind the line. It doesn't replace your daily routine or your hood cleaner.
