Why commercial only

Businesses need a different cleaner.

A home and a business need different things. A business needs the work done after hours, the same checklist every visit, and one person who answers when something's missed.

So that's all SVT Clean does. Offices, new builds, restaurant kitchens and clinics across Toronto and the GTA. No houses, no maid packages.

We show our prices before you call, because you should know what something costs before you give up your time for it.

What you can count on

In writing, before the first clean.

Insured and covered

Commercial liability insurance and WSIB coverage. Certificates on request, your business named on ours.

Checked cleaners

Every cleaner who enters your space has passed a police check.

The same crew

The same people every visit, so they learn your space and your rules.

A photo report

After every clean, a short report with photos of the work.

Missed something? We come back

Tell us within 24 hours and we re-clean it, free.

No long lock-in

Month to month, with 30 days' notice. We keep you by doing the work.
