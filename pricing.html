    How we price

    Ranges you can plan with.

    Recurring cleaning is priced by the month, from the size of the space and how often we come. Washrooms, kitchenettes, carpet and restocking move it a little. Small spaces have a minimum per visit, so a tiny office never gets a silly number.

    One-off jobs, like post-construction and kitchen deep cleans, are priced per job. Every price is plus HST.

    Questions

    Prices, answered.

    What is the going rate for an office cleaner in Toronto?

    Commercial cleaners in the GTA usually charge $30 to $60 an hour per cleaner, or a monthly price for recurring work. We price by the month, so your bill is the same every month.

    What are average commercial cleaning rates in Ontario?

    For an office cleaned 3 times a week, a mid-range firm charges about a set rate per sq ft per month. Clinics cost more, large spaces a little less per square foot.

    Why a range and not one price?

    Two offices of the same size can take very different time: layout, traffic, floors, how clean it is now. The range covers spaces like yours. The walkthrough fixes your number.

    Are there contracts?

    No long lock-in. Recurring cleaning runs month to month with 30 days' notice.
